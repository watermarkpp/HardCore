Date: 2026-10-09. Current candidate is isolated and NOT_RUN beyond the retained direct_01 parser FAIL. This document defines review and comparison requirements, not a performance result.

The user's latest direction permits less frequent, less precise AI and human-acceptable reaction waits. It does not permit regression to synchronous long-frame work, invalid collision/movement, dropped committed attacks or damage, duplicate clocks, or lost struck/action ordering. Earlier AGENTS prohibitions against reducing frequency to fabricate equivalent-load performance must be interpreted with this explicit product authorization: keep the full actor/content workload, separately disclose changed decision rate, motion, attacks and service latency, and never label fewer AI decisions an equivalent-work algorithm speedup.

Use the existing central combat/action ownership and process FrameBudget. Current bounded experiment admits due observation and a new pursuit step; existing movement, collision, action/struck progression and damage stay outside this optional gate. Necessary work must not become optional merely because AI is waiting. Conversely, denied optional planning cannot fall back to a necessary scope and evade the budget.

The quota resets on actual Engine process epochs, including multiple physics callbacks in one process iteration. Missed AI decisions do not accumulate debt. Each new decision reads the current authoritative actor/target state; do not replay obsolete player coordinates or accelerate movement using a queued delta. Pending weak identities/kinds retain fairness age while changed state is validated cheaply. Disabled, deleted, controlled or unusable-target actors cannot reserve unserviceable slots. Cancellation cannot refund already spent epoch admissions.

Diagnostics reset must clear statistics only. It cannot flush queues, ages, active scopes, spent capacity or warmup behavior at the sample boundary. Nested observation/neighbor work shares one outer planning scope; no second FrameBudget scope, duplicate charge, overwritten token, leaked borrow or await across an active scope. Do not put a full queue snapshot on every decision call.

The optional budget is an admission and accounting boundary, not preemption. A running synchronous terrain/geometry query can overrun. Retain maximum quantum and overrun evidence; if an individual operation is too large, its continuation must be bounded before claiming protection against long frames. A low average with worse p95/p99/max or a growing unserved backlog is not acceptance.
